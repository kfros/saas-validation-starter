# Stage 1 Gates

The Judge uses only evidence marked `VERIFIED` by the Evidence Auditor.

The purpose of these gates is to decide whether an idea deserves Stage 2 prospect mining and customer interviews.

## G1 — Concrete pain

Target threshold:

- at least 20 VERIFIED independent concrete pain signals;
- preferably at least 10 from plausible target ICPs rather than peripheral users.

A "pain signal" must describe an actual workflow problem, cost, delay, error, frustration, or workaround.

## G2 — Recurrence

The Judge assigns:

- HIGH confidence;
- MEDIUM confidence;
- LOW confidence;
- UNKNOWN.

PASS requires at least MEDIUM confidence that the core job recurs often enough for a SaaS relationship.

If frequency cannot be established from public evidence, the result may be CONDITIONAL PASS if all other gates are strong and frequency becomes an explicit interview question.

## G3 — Existing spend / WTP

Target threshold:

- at least 5 VERIFIED money signals;
- from at least 2 distinct categories of spend.

Examples of distinct categories:

- SaaS subscription;
- employee time;
- contractor/freelancer spend;
- agency/service spend;
- dedicated job role;
- paid adjacent software.

Five competitor pricing pages alone do not satisfy this gate.

## G4 — Repeatable gap in existing solutions

Target threshold:

- at least 10 VERIFIED evidence records showing meaningful workaround, manual cleanup, missing capability, quality failure, or workflow break;
- the evidence should cluster around one or a few repeatable problems.

Ten unrelated minor complaints are not a wedge.

## G5 — ICP reachability

Evidence should identify:

- plausible role names;
- plausible company/industry segments;
- where those people can be discovered or contacted;
- enough public market surface to make Stage 2 prospect-list creation plausible.

The Judge assigns HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / UNKNOWN reachability confidence.

PASS requires at least MEDIUM.

## G6 — No killer substitute

The Skeptic and Market research must not reveal an obvious low-friction substitute that already solves the proposed workflow sufficiently well for the target ICP at a price that destroys the planned value proposition.

If one exists:

- FAIL the current wedge, or
- explicitly redefine the wedge before proceeding.

Do not wave away a killer substitute because "our UX could be better."

## Verdict definitions

### PASS

All core gates pass with enough evidence to justify Stage 2.

### CONDITIONAL PASS

The idea has strong evidence but one important question remains unresolved and is realistically answerable through interviews. The unresolved item must be explicitly named.

### FAIL

One or more core assumptions are contradicted strongly enough that continuing the current formulation is not justified.

### INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

The research did not produce enough trustworthy evidence to decide. This is not a PASS.

## Anti-goal

Do not adjust thresholds after seeing results merely to rescue an attractive idea.
