# Stage 1 Scorecard

The Stage 1 Judge should report a structured scorecard but the gate verdict takes precedence over a summed score.

Suggested dimensions:

- pain_strength: 0-5
- recurrence_confidence: 0-5
- revealed_wtp: 0-5
- current_solution_gap: 0-5
- icp_clarity: 0-5
- reachability: 0-5
- substitute_risk: 0-5 where 5 = low risk
- evidence_quality: 0-5
- source_diversity: 0-5
- overall_confidence: 0-5

Do not manufacture decimal precision.

The output must also state:

- strongest positive evidence;
- strongest negative evidence;
- top unknowns;
- candidate ICPs;
- candidate wedges;
- Stage 2 questions.
