# Discovery leads — NOT evidence

The names and search strings below are starting leads from the idea discussion,
not verified current products, capabilities, prices, usage, or market findings.
Use approved browser/search discovery; open the exact original public page before
creating a record. Do not place this file or its statements in counted evidence.

## Competitor/substitute leads

- Otterly.AI, Rankscale, Peec AI, Profound: investigate current agency monitoring
  offers, workload limits, reporting, exports, sampling, and entry requirements.
- Existing SEO suites: investigate whether their current AI-visibility components
  are already bundled or separately billed; do not assume absence or presence.
- Manual prompt checks plus spreadsheets/client reporting; analytics/referral
  reports; one-off agency audits; outsourced monitoring/reporting services.
- Byword and Koala: adjacent content-execution alternatives only when actual buyer
  behavior makes them relevant; their writing revenue does not validate monitoring.

## Source discovery discipline

Follow original practitioner reviews, public agency posts, support threads,
actual hiring/project listings, and named buyer accounts. Record buyer/seller
interest and vendor-hosted testimonials. Syndicated claims share one origin.
Current official docs/pricing establish only the publisher's stated offer.
If source identity or date is uncertain, state that; never substitute today's date
for an unknown publication date. Use today's date only for `observed_at`.
