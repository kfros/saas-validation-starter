# Research brief — GEO-AGENCY-01

## Decision, not advocacy

Decide whether the written monitoring/reporting hypothesis deserves Stage 2
prospect mining and interviews. The prior idea-ranking score is not evidence.
No revenue, funding, traffic, growth, or price quoted in the idea pitch has been
validated by this kit. Do not seed the dataset with those claims.

One scope only: small independent SEO agencies serving recurring SMB clients.
The proposed differentiation is an inspectable, comparable client report that
supports a real decision. It is NOT assumed to be missing from current tools.
Brand owners, enterprise teams, solo consultants, and content automation are
adjacent research leads, not alternate routes to PASS in this run.

## Questions by research track

| Track | Questions and required report content |
| --- | --- |
| Market | Current direct trackers, agency plans and SEO-suite bundles; comparable workload pricing; reports/exports; historical evidence; brand/client limits; exact engine surfaces; data provenance; manual/service substitutes. Separate published capability from observed sufficiency. |
| Pain | First-hand agency events: reporting/reconciliation effort, inconsistent readings, false alerts, client disputes, missing useful actions, abandoned tools. Record user impact and attempted workaround. Include satisfied users and one-off-only needs. |
| WTP | Actual agency purchases/renewals/cancellations, monitoring-specific services, paid employee time, material dedicated duties. Split ongoing spend, one-off spend, intent, and vendor prices. Do not allocate a whole SEO/content retainer to monitoring. |
| Workflow | Trigger -> inputs -> measurement -> review -> client report -> decision; actor versus buyer; sourced recurrence; supported agency size; public discovery/contact surfaces. Include 3-5 illustrative agency examples when possible, not a full lead list and not a new numeric gate. |
| Skeptic | Cheap/bundled substitutes, low decision value, novelty churn, content-execution demand instead of monitoring, sampling instability, UI/API mismatch, data access, operating costs and support burden. Address the strongest version of existing alternatives. |

## GEO-specific traps to test, not assume resolved

- A brand mention, a cited URL, a recommendation, a referral session, and a sale
  are different outcomes. A change in one does not prove a change in another.
- A chosen prompt sample is not a known distribution of real customer questions.
  No population-wide share-of-voice or search-volume claim without a denominator.
- An API result is not evidence of the consumer ChatGPT/Gemini/etc. interface.
  Record exact product surface, retrieval/search mode, model/version if exposed,
  date, language, localization mechanism, repetitions, and aggregation method.
  A country name inside a prompt is not automatically geographic measurement.
- One changed answer does not establish a sustained trend. Investigate sampling,
  model/product changes, personalized/session effects, and reproducibility.
- A vendor may offer a feature while users still experience poor quality. Conversely,
  one complaint does not establish a permanent missing capability or market-wide pain.
- Public product pages are not proof that the underlying data route is permitted,
  reliable, or economical for our own product. Do not give a legal conclusion;
  record official documentation and unresolved commercial-access questions.
- Growth/funding of a category does not prove small-agency WTP or founder-accessible
  distribution. Cheap geography and future LTV are not assumptions that cancel CAC.

## Economics worksheet (Skeptic report; no actual service calls)

Use a clearly labelled scenario, not a forecast:

`observations/month = clients * prompts/client * surfaces * locale_variants * repetitions * checks/month`

For differing per-surface prices, sum the surface-specific observation costs.
Add evidenced retry overhead, extraction/analysis, storage, support, and any
minimum supplier fees without double-counting bundled charges. Show low/base/high
assumptions and their sources; leave unknown inputs unknown. Compare against actual
buyer prices, not only the hypothetical $100/agency/month objective. Do not invent
gross margin or CAC payback. A technical probe would be a separately authorized task.

## Output quality

Each report distinguishes facts (evidence IDs), hypotheses, and unknowns. Each
raw record is atomic, source-backed, schema-valid and `PENDING`. Use subtype to
describe job-specific phenomena; do not extend the canonical schema with new fields.
Put sampling details, source inspection notes, uncertainty and scope linkage in
the Markdown reports or the prescribed audit sidecar, referencing evidence IDs.

The targets in skills are search guides, never quotas. A complete search can
produce insufficient evidence. Scarcity is not market falsification; failures of
tooling are not evidence that no market exists.
