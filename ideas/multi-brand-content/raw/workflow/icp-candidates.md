# ICP Candidates & Reachability Analysis

## 1. Primary Scoped ICP: MULTIBRAND-OPERATOR-01

### Scope Definition
- **Target Organization:** Independent freelance social media manager (Solo) or boutique owner-led agency (1–5 employees).
- **Core Job:** External provider producing recurring organic static posts, carousels, and captions for multiple unrelated SMB clients.
- **Hands-on Operator:** The owner/operator is personally involved in drafting, reviewing, revising, or finalizing client content assets.
- **Client Profile:** Small-to-medium businesses (SMBs), local service providers, e-commerce brands, or professional services lacking an in-house creative team.

### Sub-Segment Comparison: Solo vs. Boutique Agency

| Attribute | Solo Freelancer (SOLO) | Boutique Agency Owner (AGENCY) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Typical Client Count** | 3 – 8 concurrent retainer clients | 8 – 25 concurrent retainer clients |
| **Operator Role** | Sole creator (copy + design + scheduling) | Creative director, editor, client account manager |
| **Team Size** | 1 (Sole proprietor) | 2 – 5 (Owner + 1–3 junior contractors/designers) |
| **Tool Budget Owner** | Self (pays from personal/business revenue) | Owner / Agency Principal |
| **Primary Workflow Pain** | Context-switching fatigue; time spent on repetitive resizing/reformatting in Canva | Version control drift between junior drafts and client feedback; tool costs scaling per seat/workspace |
| **Review Method** | Loom walkthroughs, shared Notion boards, direct links (`mb-workflow-001`, `mb-workflow-003`) | Decoupled approval platforms (Planable, Netgain, Recur Post) (`mb-workflow-002`, `mb-workflow-004`) |

## 2. Adjacent Candidate Segments (Explicitly Boundary-Excluded)

To maintain scope integrity, the following adjacent segments are distinguished and excluded from `MULTIBRAND-OPERATOR-01`:

1. **In-House Brand Marketing Teams:**
   - *Why Excluded:* Single-brand context; no multi-brand tenant switching or external client review approval bottlenecks.
   - *Status:* OUT OF SCOPE.
2. **Mid-to-Large Digital Agencies (50+ employees):**
   - *Why Excluded:* Departmental specialization (dedicated copywriters, art directors, traffic managers, account executives); enterprise procurement and SSO requirements.
   - *Status:* OUT OF SCOPE.
3. **Creators & Influencers:**
   - *Why Excluded:* Content produced solely for self-promotion; no client approval, client brand kits, or external deliverables.
   - *Status:* OUT OF SCOPE.

## 3. Reachability & Stage 2 Discovery Strategy

Stage 1 confirms that `MULTIBRAND-OPERATOR-01` buyers are identifiable and publicly reachable across several established professional channels.

### A. Identifiable Roles & Search Queries
- **Job / Headline Titles:**
  - "Freelance Social Media Manager"
  - "Social Media Strategist & Content Creator"
  - "Owner" OR "Founder" AND "Social Media Agency"
  - "Boutique Creative Director" AND "Organic Social"
- **Company Headcount Filters:** Self-employed (1) or Small Business (2–10 employees).

### B. Legitimate Discovery Routes for Stage 2 Prospecting

1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator / Public Search:**
   - Target queries: `title:("Social Media Manager" OR "Social Media Specialist") AND ("Freelance" OR "Consultant" OR "Agency Owner")`.
   - Filters: Company size 1–10; Industry: Marketing & Advertising; Geography: English-speaking markets (US, UK, CA, AU).
2. **Boutique Agency Directories (Clutch, The Manifest):**
   - Filter by Service: "Social Media Marketing"; Team Size: "< 10 employees"; Client Focus: "Small Business (< $10M)".
   - Provides verified agency names and owner/leadership listings.
3. **Professional Communities & Forums:**
   - Subreddits: `r/SocialMediaMarketing` (practitioners actively discussing tool pain and workflows), `r/freelance`.
   - Facebook Communities: Freelance Social Media Managers networks, The Social Media Squad.
   - Slack / Discord Communities: Freelance and indie marketer groups (e.g., Marketers in Tech, Online Geniuses).
4. **Freelance Marketplaces (Upwork Pro / Top Rated Directories):**
   - Filter by: SMM category, "Agency" or "Independent", verified billings > $10k (signaling active multi-client retainers).

### C. Exemplar Providers Observed in Research
- **QuimbyDigital** (`mb-workflow-001`): Boutique digital agency active on `r/SocialMediaMarketing`, utilizing Loom + ClickUp/Notion async approval workflows for clients.
- Note: In compliance with research rules, no direct contact, outreach, or personal data harvesting has been conducted during Stage 1.
