# Mandatory claim checks for all tracks, Auditor and Judge

Use before creating or counting a claim. This checklist fixes recurring inference
errors in previous runs. It contains rules and examples, not market evidence.

| Observed source | Supported conclusion | Unsupported upgrade to prevent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We use tool X" without payment details | Actual use for the stated job | Paid subscription, amount, tier or renewal |
| "Without X we would spend days" | Hypothetical counterfactual, vendor/customer perception | Actual historical days worked or cash saved |
| Vendor calculates N clients × M hours | Seller's scenario | Firsthand agency labor/WTP |
| SMM provider lists a service/retainer price | Published offer | Provider bought production tools or entire fee is automatable |
| End client paid an agency | Client service purchase | Same amount is the agency's software budget |
| Unawarded freelance project has a budget | Stated purchase intent | Completed contractor engagement or actual spend |
| Job listing includes content as a material duty | Dedicated-role recruitment signal | Hired person, full salary attributable to the target job |
| Owner describes real work for paying clients | Actual operator labor, amount may be unknown | Cash payment to self, hourly rate, or dollars computed from hours |
| Posts go live daily | Publishing schedule | Owner performs the production/revision job daily |
| Client replies late | Actual approval delay | Software can remove the delay or drafting is the bottleneck |
| User requests a feature | Stated preference | Experienced gap unless actual workflow impact is described |
| Official page documents a feature | Published capability | Adequate quality, target adoption, purchase or no remaining gap |
| New docs coexist with an old complaint | Time-specific facts | Old pain vanished, or old product limitation is still current |
| Provider does not bill a separate line item | Service packaging choice | Refusal to pay for internal tools |
| Team-size range or vague agency label | Exact stated range/role | Invented headcount, hands-on owner or proven ICP membership |
| Same provider has several reviews/posts | Multiple observations from one entity | Several independent demand signals |
| Social tool complaint concerns only scheduling | Adjacent publishing pain | Pain in static content production |
| Export exists | Vendor offers that format | Buyer accepts flattening or native editable files are unnecessary |

## High-impact review format

For each decisive or monetary record include:
ID; actual source URL; source inspection date/status; audit status; provider form
and scope support IDs; directly supported observation; interpretation boundary;
eligible gate(s) or exclusion reason; unresolved question.

Group duplicates but preserve IDs. Include every money signal regardless of audit
status, the strongest positive/negative records and all serious substitutes.
For money, explicitly state: payer, recipient, work bought/done, paid/free/unknown,
actual/intent/hypothetical, amount basis and what remains unknown.
For substitutes, separately state capability, same-job fit, price/friction,
observed sufficiency and the evidence needed to resolve remaining uncertainty.

## Before Judge handoff

- Every raw ID survived consolidation, with a concrete audit reason.
- No observation, amount, scope field or classification was silently rewritten.
- Scope support is VERIFIED and linked to the same actual entity.
- Repeated provider claims have a shared independence key.
- Counts match the checker's DATASET_SUMMARY, including UNKNOWN versus OUT_OF_SCOPE.
- Every promoted claim is supported at its stated strength; no "use" to "paid" upgrade.
- No missing evidence has become negative evidence.
- Current versus historical observations and commercial source interests are explicit.
- Unsupported claims remain PARTIALLY_VERIFIED/REJECTED/PENDING, not rescued in prose.
- A checker success is reported as a structural check, never proof of source truth.
