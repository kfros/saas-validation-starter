# Discovery leads — not evidence

These names and query families are search starting points, not verified current
features, prices, market shares or product recommendations. Open original public
pages before making any factual record. No search snippet is evidence.

## Alternative families to inspect

- Design/template and batch-production tools: Canva, Adobe Express.
- Social suites and planning/review tools: Buffer, Hootsuite, SocialPilot,
  SocialBee, Planable, Kontentino.
- Social-content generation tools: Predis.ai, Ocoya.
- Existing general AI + client brand documents + templates + shared folders.
- Freelance design/copy production and service providers hired by SMM operators.

Discover additional alternatives from what practitioners actually use. A named
product may be irrelevant or changed; verify before including it. Do not give
these candidates equal relevance simply because they are listed here.

Seek satisfaction and rejection as deliberately as complaints: "good enough",
"stopped paying", "free plan", "client provides assets", "only setup", "not worth",
"editing takes longer", "already included". Do not restrict research to AI-tool users.

## Preferred source families

Original practitioner posts, review permalinks, support threads, provider workflow
writeups, actual hiring/project records and named accounts of paid tool use.
Original provider service/team/contact pages can supply atomic scope/reachability
records; service offerings alone do not establish pain or purchase.

A seller's article about what agencies "would spend" is not a firsthand buyer.
Customer logos, vendor ARR, funding and list prices do not establish target WTP.
If a useful source is inaccessible, record the blocker in notes; do not turn the
snippet or an internal browser artifact into supported evidence.
