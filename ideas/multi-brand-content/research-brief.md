# Research brief — Multi-brand production

All proposed pains, workflow steps, formats and benefits below are hypotheses.
Do not copy them into evidence. Test scope MULTIBRAND-OPERATOR-01 as written.

## The decision

Does this exact production/revision job deserve Stage 2 prospect mining and
interviews? Stage 1 does not validate a finished product, set a launch budget,
authorize development or decide the future of ChillPup.

The ranking score from the idea discussion is not evidence or a prior gate result.

## What to investigate

| Track | Main question | Required distinctions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market | What already covers the same production workflow? | Capability, plan limits, output fit, price, paid use and satisfaction are separate facts. |
| Pain | What concrete production/revision problem have matching providers experienced? | Context switching and cleanup versus generic workload, feature wishes or scheduling failures. |
| WTP | Who already commits money or labor to this job? | Provider spending versus their clients' service fees; actual paid use versus free use or intent. |
| Workflow | What starts and finishes the job, who does it, and how often? | Production cycle versus publishing cadence; required handoff versus our preferred export. |
| Skeptic | Why would a provider stay with existing tools or reject this workflow? | Good-enough substitutes, input/strategy bottlenecks, quality burden, switching and delivery economics. |

## Proposed workflow and limits

Client-approved brief/assets → brand-specific draft → copy/layout review →
revision → approved content pack handed to existing publishing tools.

This is a workflow hypothesis. Do not invent a sequence, number of edits,
formats, monthly volume, headcount or time saved. Discover whether static images,
carousels, captions and editable exports form one purchased job or different jobs.

Potential clusters to test: repeatedly restoring client brand context; correcting
layout/copy across a batch; propagating a client revision; checking the right
assets and version before handoff. Search outside these phrases so the proposed
solution does not determine the discovered pain.

Late client approval is not automatically automatable: determine whether the
cause is missing information, decision latency, unclear scope or software rework.
A tool cannot be assumed to solve client indecision or create unavailable assets.

Scheduling/API failures, generic social-media burnout, engagement decline,
client acquisition, stock-image demand and video editing are adjacent context
unless a specific observation links them to the defined production job.

## Buyer, operator and payer

The provider sells services to clients and buys tools/services to deliver them.
Those are different transactions. A client's large SMM retainer is not proof
the provider will pay that amount for production software.

Record actual tool payment, contractor engagement or labor on the mapped job.
A source can establish a useful behavior with unknown amount. Do not infer a
paid tier from a logo, a product name, a case study or the existence of pricing.
Vendor-hosted firsthand testimony can support what it explicitly says, with
commercial interest disclosed; marketing arithmetic cannot establish actual labor.

Owner time on real paid client production may be recorded as employee_time with
subtype owner_operator_time, because the canonical schema has no separate owner
category. Explain this mapping; it is labor evidence, not an observed cash payment.
Keep money_amount, money_currency and money_period null unless a monetary amount
for that exact work is directly sourced. Hours belong in observation.

An unfilled job listing is recruitment evidence, not proof of historical payroll.
A material dedicated role can be retained under the canonical gate convention,
but identify that weaker signal separately. Never assign its whole salary to
post production. Unawarded projects/budgets remain stated_wtp context.

## Scope evidence without invented precision

Establish the external service relationship, multiple client brands, recurring
static production, owner involvement and SMB clientele. Use the original record
or explicit same-entity profile records. Exact size, country and monthly volume
are descriptive when available, not new inclusion hurdles.

A profile saying only "social media manager" leaves scope UNKNOWN. A range such
as 10–49 employees must stay that range. Do not manufacture a narrower estimate.
Record provider form as SOLO, AGENCY or UNKNOWN in the audit sidecar. Provider
form alone is not enough for scope, and unknown form alone does not exclude a
source whose operational scope is otherwise fully supported.

Keep operator pain separate from proof of budget authority. A founder profile
plus a staff testimonial needs a demonstrated link to the same actual job.
Do not infer hands-on ownership merely because a team has a founder.

## Substitutes and operating feasibility

Compare existing design/template tools, general AI plus templates, social suites,
approval tools and outsourced production as actual stacks. A combined existing
stack can be sufficient even if no single product has every proposed feature.
Conversely, an official capability does not establish adequate quality or adoption.

Investigate these questions through current official sources and firsthand use:

- Brand isolation, reusable templates, batch operations, version/revision propagation.
- Editable outputs and file compatibility; whether export is accepted by the buyer.
- Client reviewer access, seat/client/brand limits and incremental versus total cost.
- Supported integrations and asset usage terms relevant to the required handoff.
- Setup, review, corrections and support that remain after generation.

Do not turn absence on one feature page into proof a feature is absent.
An old complaint and a newer capability may both be true; establish dates and
explain what remains unresolved. "AI content is bad" is not universal evidence.

Skeptic's economics worksheet must separate buyer labor from our delivery cost:
client brands × approved deliverables/brand × billable generation/render attempts,
plus fixed provider fees, storage and support where sourced. Label workload and
revision assumptions as scenarios; show unknowns instead of fabricated margins.
Compare at the same output and workload. No paid calls, trial signups or live
benchmarks are authorized. Missing cost data is an uncertainty, not proof of failure.

## Reporting discipline

Each material sentence in a report identifies evidence IDs or labels an assumption
or UNKNOWN. Record source family/query coverage and disconfirming search attempts.
Counts measure independent entities/events as defined by the protocol; multiple
pages from the same provider cannot manufacture demand.

Use fixed canonical gates. These are prioritization rules, not statistically
calibrated estimates of market demand. A sparse public dataset can justify
INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE; it does not prove the category has no buyers.
