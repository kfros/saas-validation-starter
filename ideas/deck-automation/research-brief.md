# Research Brief — Deck Automation

## Core workflow under investigation

A business user takes a master/company presentation plus opportunity-specific information and creates a customer-specific deck suitable for an external client or decision-maker.

Potential inputs may include:

- prior company decks;
- corporate PowerPoint templates;
- CRM/opportunity information;
- discovery-call notes/transcripts;
- product/service documentation;
- customer website or public information;
- pricing, case studies or proof points.

Potential output:

- on-brand, accurate, customer-specific, editable PPTX.

These inputs and outputs are hypotheses, not facts. Research must determine whether they match actual workflows.

## High-value evidence

Prioritize evidence about:

- time spent customizing decks;
- how often customization happens;
- manual copy/paste or slide selection;
- maintaining approved/current slides;
- PowerPoint/PPTX as required deliverable;
- broken or low-quality export from AI presentation products;
- corporate-template requirements;
- presentation agencies/freelancers or internal roles paid to do this work;
- security/privacy concerns;
- usage or rejection of Gamma, Copilot, Pitch, Canva, Beautiful.ai, Plus AI and adjacent tools;
- sales-enablement systems that already automate parts of the workflow.

## Do not assume

Do not assume that account executives make several decks per day, that PowerPoint export is always required, or that teams will pay USD 100+ per month. These are questions to research.
